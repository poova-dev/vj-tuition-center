Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information parents and students ask regarding admissions, classes, and batches.

We offer comprehensive coaching starting from Kindergarten (KG) & Primary (Classes 1–5), Middle School (Classes 6–8), High School (Classes 9 & 10 - SSLC / CBSE), and Higher Secondary (Classes 11 & 12 - Science, Commerce & Computer Science streams).
We provide dedicated subject tuition for Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science, English Grammar & Literature, Social Science, Tamil, and Commerce/Accountancy. Both all-subject combined packages and individual subject modules are available.
You can submit an online Admission Enquiry directly on this website, call our administrative office at +91 94431 23456, or visit our Kumbakonam center during office hours (Mon-Sat 6 AM - 8:30 PM). A brief student diagnostic interaction is conducted to assign the most appropriate batch.
We operate convenient morning and evening batches to suit school schedules: Morning batches run from 6:00 AM – 8:00 AM, and Evening batches run from 4:30 PM – 8:30 PM. Special weekend revision and test series batches are held on Saturdays and Sundays.
You can reach us by phone at +91 94431 23456, message us on WhatsApp, or email us at admissions@vjtuitionkumbakonam.com. Our institution is located centrally at TSR Big Street, Kumbakonam, Tamil Nadu 612001.
