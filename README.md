# V.J Tuition Center, Kumbakonam — Official Website Demo

A premium, modern, fully responsive educational institution website demo built for **V.J Tuition Center**, Kumbakonam.

## 🎓 About the Institution
- **Institution**: V.J Tuition Center
- **Location**: TSR Big Street, Kumbakonam, Tamil Nadu
- **Founder**: Sriram J
- **Positioning**: Quality academic guidance from KG to 12th standard (State Board & CBSE), fostering conceptual depth, student achievement, and institutional credibility.

---

## 🚀 Pages Included
1. **Home (`index.html`)** — Sticky navigation, hero section, achievement stats, academic programs, why choose us, founder spotlight, results preview, faculty preview, gallery preview, Google reviews highlight, and admission CTA.
2. **About Us (`about.html`)** — Institution history, Founder's vision & mission, teaching philosophy, and modern campus infrastructure.
3. **Classes & Programs (`classes.html`)** — KG & Primary, Middle School (6-8), Secondary (9-10), Higher Secondary (11-12), and weekly batch schedule.
4. **Results & Achievements (`results.html`)** — State Board & CBSE board exam track record, centum scorers, student spotlights, and annual awards.
5. **Faculty (`faculty.html`)** — Subject department heads and experienced mentor profiles with qualifications and teaching experience.
6. **Gallery (`gallery.html`)** — Interactive photo gallery with category filtering (Classrooms, Events, Achievements, Students, Activities) and a fullscreen lightbox with keyboard navigation.
7. **Careers (`careers.html`)** — Job opportunities ("Build Your Career With Us"), role details modal, and interactive application form.
8. **Reviews (`reviews.html`)** — Google Reviews style showcase (4.8 rating), detailed rating breakdown, and verified parent/student testimonials.
9. **FAQ (`faq.html`)** — Accessible JavaScript accordion covering admissions, syllabi, timings, fee guidance, and progress tracking.
10. **Contact Us (`contact.html`)** — Interactive enquiry form, direct phone & WhatsApp links, location details, and embedded Google Maps view.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **HTML5** — Clean, semantic, and accessible markup.
- **Tailwind CSS** — Modern typography, responsive layouts, and curated institutional color palette (Oxford Navy `#0A2540`, Royal Indigo `#1E3A8A`, Warm Amber `#D97706`).
- **Vanilla JavaScript** — High-performance interactive components (modals, drawers, lightbox, accordion, toast alerts).
- **Lucide Icons** — Consistent SVG iconography.
- **Google Fonts** — Outfit & Plus Jakarta Sans typography.
- **LocalStorage** — Demo form state persistence (Admission Enquiries, Job Applications, Contact Messages).
- **Mobile UX** — Fixed mobile action bar (Home, Call, WhatsApp, Enquire) with zero horizontal overflow.
