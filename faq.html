Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our classes, subjects, timings, faculty, and admission process in Kumbakonam.

We provide structured academic coaching across all key school stages:
  • KG & Primary: Kindergarten through 5th Standard (Basic math, reading, handwriting, Tamil, English).
  • Middle School: Classes 6, 7 and 8 (Conceptual foundation in mathematics, integrated science, and grammar).
  • High School: Classes 9 and 10 (Tamil Nadu State Board SSLC and CBSE 10th Board preparation).
  • Higher Secondary: Classes 11 and 12 (+1 & +2 Higher Secondary in Science, Biology-Maths, Computer Science, and Commerce streams).
Subject specialists are available for:
  • Mathematics: Algebra, Trigonometry, Geometry, Matrices, Calculus, Business Math.
  • Science: Physics (mechanics, optics, electrostatics), Chemistry (organic, physical, inorganic), Biology (Botany & Zoology).
  • Languages: English Grammar & Literature, Tamil language and composition.
  • Social Studies: History, Geography, Civics, Economics for class 9 & 10.
  • Commerce Stream: Accountancy, Commerce, Business Studies, Economics.
Parents can choose all-subject integrated batches or individual subject coaching modules.
Admissions are streamlined and parent-friendly:
  1. Submit an online enquiry using the Admission Enquiry button on this website, or call our office at +91 94431 23456.
  2. Visit our center on TSR Big Street, Kumbakonam with your child's recent report card.
  3. Our academic director conducts a short diagnostic interaction to identify your child's current learning level and assign the most suitable batch.
  4. Enrollment is confirmed upon batch allotment.
We schedule batches comfortably around school hours:
  • Morning Batch: 6:00 AM – 8:00 AM (Ideal for 10th & 12th board students for fresh-mind problem practice).
  • Primary Batch: 5:00 PM – 6:30 PM (Classes 1 to 5).
  • Evening Regular Batch: 5:30 PM – 7:30 PM (Middle school & High school).
  • Late Evening / Senior Batch: 6:30 PM – 8:30 PM (Classes 11 & 12).
  • Sunday Revision Tests: 8:00 AM – 11:30 AM (Weekly mock examinations and doubt solving).

Unlike overcrowded coaching classes with 60+ students, we strictly limit each classroom batch to approximately 15 to 18 students (and 12 for primary classes). This ensures our teachers make eye contact with every student, check their daily homework copies, and immediately spot when a child is struggling.
Every weekend, students take chapter-wise evaluation tests. Evaluated answer sheets are returned within 4 days with line-by-line teacher remarks. Parents receive monthly progress summaries and are invited for 1-on-1 parent-teacher interactions before major school examinations.
