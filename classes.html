KG to 12th Standard

Classes & Academic Programs

Structured curricula aligned with Tamil Nadu State Board (Samacheer Kalvi) and CBSE standards, customized for every stage of schooling.

Foundation Wing

KG & Primary (Classes 1–5)

Primary education sets the tone for a student's entire academic life. Our primary coaching builds love for books, rock-solid fundamental math skills, clear phonics and reading comprehension, and neat handwriting without fear of judgment.

Core Curriculum:

English Grammar & Phonics, Tamil, Basic Mathematics & Tables, General Science & Environmental Studies.

Batch Features:

Max 12 students per batch, daily homework assistance, memory games, and supportive female educators.

Timings: 5:00 PM – 6:30 PM (Mon to Fri)
Analytical Stage

Middle School (Classes 6, 7 & 8)

Middle school is where foundational arithmetic transforms into algebraic thinking, and general science divides into specialized physics, chemistry, and biology. We train young minds to reason logically and articulate structured answers.

Core Curriculum:

Mathematics (Fractions, Algebra, Geometry), Integrated Science, Social Science, English Grammar, Tamil.

Batch Features:

Bi-weekly chapter tests, concept visualizers, school exam revision, and parent progress tracking.

Timings: 5:30 PM – 7:30 PM (Mon to Sat)
Board Preparation
Board Milestone

Classes 9 & 10 (SSLC & CBSE 10th)

The Class 10 Board exam is the first crucial academic benchmark in a student's life. We run a rigorous, time-tested preparation system featuring chapter-wise drills, previous 10-year question solving, and answer paper presentation techniques to guarantee top scores.

Core Curriculum:

Mathematics, Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Social Science, English, Tamil (State Board).

Special Highlights:

Centum targeting program, 5 full-syllabus model board exams, formula revision booklets.

Batches: Morning (6:00 AM) & Evening (6:00 PM)
Higher Secondary Wing

Classes 11 & 12 (+1 & +2 Higher Secondary)

Specialized stream-based coaching handled by experienced postgraduate subject faculty. Intensive focus on derivations, numericals, chemical mechanisms, organic conversions, and accountancy ledgers.

Science Stream:

Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science (State Board & CBSE).

Commerce Stream:

Accountancy, Commerce, Economics, Business Mathematics & Computer Applications.
